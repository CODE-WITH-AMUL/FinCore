02 The problem

Growing a business shouldn't mean losing control of its finances.

Most small businesses don't fail because they can't sell. They struggle because the financial picture is scattered across too many tools, too late to act on.

▥SpreadsheetMultiple versions
▦Bank portalSeparate logins

Scattered information

02

Unclear cash position

You know your revenue. Where the cash actually is — and where it's going — is harder to answer.

03

Manual work

Spreadsheets and disconnected tools create hours of unnecessary administrative work each month.

04

Difficult decisions

10 FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is FinCore?

FinCore is business finance software for Nepal's small service businesses and agencies. It brings sales, expenses, invoices, and reports into one place so you can see how your business is doing.

Is FinCore really free?

Yes. The core finance features are free and do not expire. There is an optional premium tier from Rs3,000/year for businesses that need more advanced capabilities.

Who is FinCore for?

Small service businesses and agencies in Nepal — including consultants, creative studios, and growing teams that want clear financial records without a dedicated accounting department.

Can I track sales and expenses?

Yes. Record sales and expenses as they happen, assign categories, and see them reflected in your reports.

Can I create invoices?

Yes. Generate invoices, track their status, and record payments against them.

Can I track unpaid invoices?

Yes. Outstanding and overdue invoices are visible in your dashboard so nothing slips through.

Can I generate financial reports?

Yes. Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow reports are generated from the records you enter in FinCore.

What does the premium tier include?

The premium tier is being developed. It will unlock advanced capabilities beyond the free core, priced from Rs3,000/year. Final feature list and pricing will be published before launch.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You can start using FinCore's free plan without entering payment details.

Where is my data stored?

Details about hosting and data handling will be published on the security page. Contact us if you need specifics before signing up.
