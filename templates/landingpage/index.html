02 The problem

Growing a business shouldn't mean losing control of its finances.

Most businesses don't fail because they can't sell. They struggle because the financial picture is scattered across too many tools, too late to act on.

▤ Invoices 14 open
▥ Spreadsheet 3 versions
▦ Bank portal 2 logins
▧ Expense app Unreconciled
scattered across 4 tools · 2 people · 6 days/month
01

Scattered information

Sales, expenses, invoices, and payments live in different places. Nothing reconciles to anything else.

02

Unclear cash position

You know your revenue. Where the cash actually is — and where it's going — is harder to answer.

03

Manual work

Spreadsheets and disconnected tools create hours of unnecessary administrative work each month.

04

Difficult decisions

Without a clear financial picture, planning hiring, pricing, and investment becomes guesswork.

{# ============ 19 · FAQ ============ #}
11 FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is FinCore?

FinCore is a business financial management platform. It brings sales, expenses, invoices, payments, cash flow, and reporting into one system so growing businesses can understand their numbers.

Who is FinCore for?

Startups, small businesses, agencies, freelancers, and growing companies that want clear financial visibility without a complicated accounting stack.

Can I track sales and expenses?

Yes. Record sales and expenses as they happen, categorize them, and see them reflected in reports immediately.

Can I create invoices?

Yes. Generate and send invoices from FinCore, track their status, and record payments against them.

Can I track unpaid invoices?

Yes. Outstanding and overdue invoices are visible in the dashboard, with aging shown so nothing slips.

Can I manage products?

Yes. Add products and services to your catalog and attach them to sales and invoices.

Can I generate financial reports?

Yes. Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and business performance reports are generated from your actual activity.

Can I track cash flow?

Yes. See incoming and outgoing money side by side, with a live view of your cash position across accounts.

Is FinCore suitable for startups?

Yes — FinCore is designed with startups in mind, from tracking funding and burn to understanding revenue and margins as you grow.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan covers individuals and early-stage businesses. You can upgrade to Pro or Business at any time.
